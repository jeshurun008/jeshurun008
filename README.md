I'm a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at **Chennai Institute of Technology** focused on building modern full-stack applications that are scalable, performant, and designed for real-world use.

My primary interests lie in backend architecture, frontend engineering, databases, and creating products that solve practical problems. Alongside software development, I produce electronic music under the artist name **MC QD**.
